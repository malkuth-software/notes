# Plan Paint notes

This is the `plan-paint` branch of `malkuth-software/notes`: everything about Plan Paint that is worth keeping but isn't needed to build it. The product lives in [malkuth-software/plan-paint](https://github.com/malkuth-software/plan-paint).

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `interviews/` | the owner's interview pages and answer files, with the studies they rest on |

## Contents

- `interviews/icon-editor/`: the icon production custodial interview (20 October 2026). Open `mockups.html` for the interview page; `README.md` describes the portfolio. `answers.json` is the owner's exported answer record (Q03 and Q04 answered B, every other question A). `CAPABILITIES.md`, `SPECIFICATIONS.md`, `EVIDENCE.md` and `CONSERVATION.md` are the capability inventory, behavioural contracts, evidence register and control conservation ledger; `INTERVIEW.md` is the text question reference. The implemented workflows are documented in the product's `docs/ICON_EDITING.md`.

Moved from the product's `docs/icon-editor-study/` on 2026-10-27, with links to product documents made absolute and the app icon bundled beside the page. The receipts and screenshots behind the study are archived privately, not here.
