Tracer bakes

Four control-sized scenes, each given to the owner's engines in the geometry that engine takes. Every cell holds two linear radiance planes at unit white light (albedo included): F for the one fixed light, L for the lamp. They are composited here as rgb = fixed colour × power × F + lamp colour × power × L, then 1 − e−1.3v as in GUI.Forms light planes, so colours and powers never re-bake. The engines use different units and framings; "normalise" scales each plane by its own 99th percentile so that looks can be compared. Times are medians of three whole engine runs at 1, 4 and all threads.
