Tracer bakes

Four control-sized scenes lit three ways. Each cell is composited here from linear light planes baked once at unit white: rgb = albedo × (fixed colour × power × F + lamp colour × power × L), then 1 − e−1.3v as in GUI.Forms light planes. Colours and powers never re-bake. Times are the generator's medians of three runs, at 1, 4 and all threads.

No baked planes found. Build and run the generator beside this page: mkdir -p build/tracer_bakes && c++ -std=c++20 -O2 -ffp-contract=off -pthread -o build/tracer_bakes_gen tracer_bakes.cpp && build/tracer_bakes_gen build/tracer_bakes 160, then reload.
