Signal meter

A dBm meter. The bfft photonic transport engine (--scene meter) renders the recessed box under a weak ambient light and the scale, ticks and numerals glowing white with their light on the face. Over that render the page adds the face's texture and simulates the two readouts as backlit segment LCDs. Only the moving parts redraw: the three needles (signal yellow, average red, noise floor) passing beneath the readouts, and the lit segments.

Over it, composed once: the face's fine grain and faint mottle, applied as a modulation of the engine's light and kept off the scale; and the two readouts, simulated as backlit segment LCDs with an edge-lit backlight, a vignette, the matte film's grain, a dark glass edge and ghost segments. Live: the needles, and the lit segments with their parallax shadows. Nothing is redrawn while the needles rest.
