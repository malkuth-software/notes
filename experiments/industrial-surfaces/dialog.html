Process control dialog

Every lit element is rendered by the bfft photonic transport engine. CANCEL is white translucent plastic with red lamps behind it, and OK is clear green acrylic over a frosted back (--scene key, the lens reconstructed from a control grid by stx_conv). The status lights are faceted glass jewels with a bulb inside and a mirror back (--key-style jewel), seen through dots in the panel. Each key's press is 8 engine frames, stored as deltas, with a separate faint fill light so unlit plastic and glass still read. The body is the glint kernel over ГОСТ 22133-86 сине-зелёный 397. Nothing is drawn past a control's edge, and only what changes is redrawn: a pressed key, the blinking FAULT jewel.
