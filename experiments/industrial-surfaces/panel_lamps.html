Panel lamps

Captions and indicators lit from within, rendered in C++ (cpp/lamps.cpp) with the bfft participating-child law. Captions come with a clear or an opaque shell, sidewalls blocked or open, and the legend in black or white ink on the lit field, or lit letters on a dead front. Each half of a caption has its own lamp and its own colour. A frame is tone(room + Σ power × colour × lamp), with glare added once to each lamp's buffer. Click a caption to press it and toggle it.
