Lit acrylic keys

Elevated acrylic keycaps lit from within by a glowing rod, rendered in C++ (cpp/lit_key.cpp) with the participating-child law of the bfft photonic transport engine. Each grade and press state is rendered once into two buffers: the room's light, and the rod's light at unit white power. Each frame here is just tone(room + power × colour × rod), so colour, blinking and brightness cost nothing to change. Click a key to toggle it; the press blends between the two rendered states.
