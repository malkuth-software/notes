Industrial paint study

Soviet machine-tool paints, taken from the sources collected in research/soviet_paints.md. The main colours are the ones ГОСТ 22133-86 gives measured values for; a second, separate set is a hue guide only. A painted surface looks painted because its colour varies in small ways: coats from different batches, uneven film thickness, sun fading, sags, grime in the seams and wear down to the primer. Each of those layers is worked out per pixel, in Oklab, on top of a finish from the finishes page.

Soviet enamel standards name a colour by a pair of swatches in the «Картотека образцов (эталонов) цвета лакокрасочных материалов». The pair bounds the tolerance of one colour; the number shown is the first of the pair. Value classes: a means the numbers come from a standard; b means a secondary source, here the laz-legend.ru RGB list of card numbers, whose method is not stated; c means only a name or number is known, so there is no chip.

Class a: ГОСТ 22133-86 colours

ГОСТ 22133-86 Appendix 2, Table 7 gives CIE x, y and reflectance for 12 samples, measured on the «Радуга-2» instrument under illuminant C with the specular component included. The hex values convert those numbers to sRGB (Bradford adaptation C to D65, IEC 61966-2-1). Because the specular component is included, a glossy enamel looks slightly darker and more saturated than these values. White and black are set only by control samples with a reflectance bound (white at least 75 %, black at most 7 %), so they have no chip.

Class a: which colours go together

ГОСТ 22133-86 Appendix 4, Table 11 recommends two-colour schemes. It prefers pairs of the same hue at different lightness, and at most three colours on one machine, not counting signal colours.

Appendix 5 lists hammer-finish enamels МЛ-165 (ГОСТ 12034-77) to go with the main colours. They are set by control samples only (class c), so they have names but no values:

Signal colours (ГОСТ 12.4.026-76) are separate from the three-colour limit. That standard lists the card numbers that qualify (red 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 19, 37, 43, 62; yellow 216, 218, 220, 221, 254, 255, 285, 286, 287; blue 408, 409, 423, 424, 449, 450, 474, 485, 486; green 324, 325, 329, 385) and chromaticity regions, but no per-sample values, so they are not shown as chips.

Class b: hue guides from an unverified list

These are not references. The enamel standards (ПФ-115, ПФ-133, НЦ-132, МЛ-12, МЛ-152, ХВ-124) give only names and card numbers for these colours. The hex values are the laz-legend.ru RGB list, method unstated. Compared with ГОСТ 22133-86 on the swatches both cover, that list agrees within about 0.01 in x, y on light colours, runs cooler and greener on greys and is far off on dark brown. Use these for hue and ordering only.
