Paint from photographs

Paint colours measured from your control-room photographs. Each sample is a rectangle drawn on its photo. The colour is the per-channel median in linear light, so labels, specks and glare don't drag it. The spread is the standard deviation of Oklab lightness and chroma inside the rectangle, which tells us how much real paint varies. Each photo is white-balanced from a neutral patch in the same photo (marked N). Both raw and balanced values are shown, because film stock, lamps and age all tint these photographs.
