Against the request

  1. Translucent, lit green. Untinted clear acrylic; the green comes only from the lamp.
  2. On only while pressed. The lamp's power is 0 until the key is held. When off it is lit by the room (a white softbox), so it reads as clear acrylic, not a black void.
  3. Keycap shape. Elevated (sidewall 0.24), tapered sides, rounded corners, a soft quarter-round chamfer. The lens is reconstructed from a 37 × 10 control grid by stx_conv.
  4. Illuminator. One long, sparse horizontal coil just above a frosted back, suspended in the acrylic. Its light diffuses off the back, and the faces show it reflected (the row of coil images along the front).
  5. Legend. Black ink on the lit field, flat on the key's face (strokes overlap at their joints; no round caps).
  6. No leaking. Only the key's own pixels are drawn; the coverage comes from the engine.
  7. Press. 8 engine frames from up to down, stored as deltas, played over about 70 ms.
  8. Rendered from within, by the engine. The page only composites: lamp × power + room, toned as the engine tones.
  9. Glass and acrylic. The softbox's gloss across the top and along the chamfer, refraction, internal reflections. Not done: caustics on the surroundings, because the key draws nothing beyond its own edge.
