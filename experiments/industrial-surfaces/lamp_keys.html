Lamp keys

The dialog's OK and CANCEL in the panel lamps' own renderer (a copy of it, cpp/lamp_keys.cpp), with what the engine renders taught. Press CANCEL to toggle it; OK lights only while held.

Panel lampsLamp keys

What this page does

  • CANCEL: white opaque plastic, red lamps, with a soft brighter area round its lamps. OK: clear acrylic lit as the panel's ANTI-ICE caption: its lamp just above a frosted back that gives back only the lamp's light, so it is dark when off and brightest round the lamp, without a distinct wire, when on.
  • Legends: grey ink on CANCEL, white ink on OK (grey against the lit field).
  • Shape by rule: height 0.57 of the short side; the sides lean 3° + 17° × clamp((share − 0.25) / 0.75), here 10°. The camera is tilted 16° toward the viewer.
  • The tops carry a fine orange-peel and grain texture on their normal.
