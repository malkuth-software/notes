## Findings the owner should see first

1. **Drawing an image on Windows costs 145–166 ms per full-window paint on
   Shadow** (M1), against 1.2 ms for a plain fill. Pages are images. This is
   paid at launch, on every page turn and on every resize, and it is why
   Windows spends half the launch ceiling before any text exists.
2. **The X11 host repaints the whole window on every event-loop wake after an
   expose** (M6). It is why Linux does not idle at zero, and it means every
   key and pointer event costs a full repaint there.
3. **A registered image is held twice on Windows** (M5): a page at device
   resolution costs twice its pixels.
4. **macOS is the tightest platform before anything is added**: 227 ms to
   first frame and 77–80 MiB resident on the CI runner.

## House-style review

Scope reviewed by reading, against `planning/PROGRAMMING_HOUSE_STYLE.md`:
every file under `src/` and `tests/` (28 C++ files), and the nine Python files
under `scripts/` and `packaging/`.

- C++: explicit types throughout; no `auto`, lambdas, arrow access, structured
  bindings, ranges, coroutines or defaulted comparisons (the checker reports 0
  findings, and I read for them as well). Callbacks are named functors with
  named fields and stated lifetimes; borrowed `Window*` pointers say who owns
  the window. Results are named before they are returned. Failure leaves
  destinations unchanged and the tests check it. Platform types stay in the
  per-platform files.
- Python: named functions, explicit loops, annotated variables; no
  comprehensions and no lambdas.

Remaining violations and departures, known:

- `scripts/check-style.py` keeps the upstream's one-line regular expressions
  and an f-string; it is an adaptation, kept close to its source.
- Tests return some expressions directly from small helpers and use
  `reinterpret_cast` to write fixture bytes.
- `packaging/package_macos.py` and `package_linux.py` have run only in CI; I
  could not step through them locally.
- The toolkit's sources are not first-party and were not reviewed for style.

The checker is a review aid. This review is mine alone and was not checked by
a second reader.
