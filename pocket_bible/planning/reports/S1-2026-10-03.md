## 3. Findings the owner should see first

OBSERVED or MEASURED; each is detailed in the file named.

1. **The Peshitta text is not open.** ETCBC's repository is MIT for its
   program only; the Syriac text is CC BY-NC 4.0, the Leiden edition by OCR
   from Brill's 1987 printing. The register's row was wrong. A translation made
   from it would inherit the non-commercial condition. (`sources/README.md`.)
2. **Psalm 87:7 in the Syriac says something else.** Reading, labelled
   HYPOTHESIS and unaudited: “The great ones who dwell in you shall rejoice,
   and all who have been humbled in you.” No singers, no pipers, no springs.
   It shares dwelling and rejoicing with the Greek against the Hebrew.
   (`sources/MEASUREMENTS.md` §5.) The owner's proving psalm no longer rests
   on a guess; it rests on one model's reading.
3. **Portsmouth cannot set the BSB as built.** It has no en dash (2,244 uses),
   and no small capitals, old-style figures or kerning. Rejected as built; it
   is the owner's to fix.
4. **Hebrew has one cleared face, in two cuts** (Ezra SIL and Ezra SIL SR),
   which pass all 6,536 distinct sequences of the Hebrew text. Frank Ruhl
   Libre and David Libre have no cantillation; Cardo has no anchors for it;
   Noto Serif Hebrew misses three real sequences.
5. **STEPBible's brief Hebrew definitions need Online Bible's permission** by
   the file's own header. Excluded; BDB and Strong supply Hebrew definitions.
6. **MACULA's semantic domains are not ours to pass on** (“used with
   permission”). That removes the open source for search channel S006 B.
7. **The BSB has its own words-of-Jesus markup** (1,420 spans). The WEB is a
   second witness for red letters, not the only source.
8. **The two Greek texts are close.** By letters, 99.26% of the Berean base's
   138,131 words have a counterpart in order in TAGNT's Nestle-Aland side;
   1,019 Berean words and 534 STEPBible words need a mark. A further 9,102
   differ only in accent, breathing or capital.
9. **No clean text exists** for Lightfoot's Greek, Payne Smith, Trench,
   Moulton–Milligan or the Psalter *iuxta Hebraeos*: public domain, but only
   uncorrected OCR. Three of the lexica chosen in L001 (Payne Smith, Trench,
   Moulton–Milligan) are therefore not cleared, and the Intermediate Liddell
   was not located.
10. **Completeness holds.** BSB: 66 books, 1,189 chapters, 31,086 verses. It
    lacks exactly 16 verses against the KJV and carries each in a footnote;
    it differs from STEPBible's numbering at exactly the three places TVTMS
    lists.

## 9. House-style review

Scope reviewed: `tools/fetch_sources.py` (453 lines) and `sources/audit/`
(five C++ files, about 2,100 lines), read in full against
`PROGRAMMING_HOUSE_STYLE.md`; `games/scripts/check-style.py` run over the five
C++ files as an aid: 0 findings (“Semantic review still required”).

- C++: no `auto`, no lambdas, no arrow access, no structured bindings, no
  ranges pipelines, no defaulted comparisons; types spelled; named comparator;
  effects in separate statements.
- Python: named functions, explicit loops, no comprehensions, no anonymous
  callbacks; variables annotated.

Remaining violations and weaknesses, not fixed:
- `verse_inventory.cpp` uses one conditional expression (`count_words`), and
  duplicates a small USFM marker scanner that `text_inventory.cpp` also has;
  `greek_difference.cpp` repeats two helpers from `verse_inventory.cpp`. Left
  as repetition between separate audit tools; the importers will share one.
- `font_coverage.cpp` reports a table offset outside the file as a warning
  and continues with what it read, rather than failing the font. Acceptable
  for an audit of trusted, pinned fonts; **not** acceptable in a parser the
  product ships (Stage 13's parser review).
- Sizes are `std::size_t` and counts `int` or `long long` without wrapper
  types; glyph identifiers have a `typedef`, code points use `char32_t`.
- `fetch_sources.py` annotates the hash object as `"hashlib._Hash"`, a private
  name, for want of a public one.
- Throwaway scripts used to edit files during the session were kept under
  `.build/` and are not tracked; they were not written to the house style.
- No tests exist for the audit tools. They were checked against each other
  (three independent files giving 31,102 slots; `\v` marker count equal to the
  verse count) and by reading samples, not by unit tests.
