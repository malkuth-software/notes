# Later Christian writers on hard spiritual subjects (O004 note; OPEN-4)

The owner: “we should also possibly include later philosophical writings by
important christians, just as we do later rabbis. especially for those which
delve into important or difficult spiritual subjects. but not luther!”

CANDIDATE list; see `README.md` in this directory. Nothing fetched; licences as
understood. Two questions are the owner's: **which**, and **how much of each**
(OPEN-4). The list is arranged by the difficulty each writer takes on, because
that is the stated test for admission, and each entry names the smallest part
that carries the argument, since the whole of all of them is a library.

## Not Luther

GIVEN. Nothing by Luther is proposed here or among the hymns. The exclusion is
recorded so that a later session does not add him by habit.

## What the owner is asked

1. Strike and add.
2. How much of each: the part named, less, or the whole work. A working
   ceiling would help — for example, no extract longer than Mark's Gospel.
3. Whether a work with no free English (entry 9) is translated here or left out.
4. Whether these sit in one section after the Apostolic Fathers, or each beside
   the subject it treats.
