## The verse commentators (C009)

Round 001 admitted Rashi, Ibn Ezra, Ramban and Malbim on the same test — only
where a comment opens the verse. No passages are proposed here: choosing them
verse by verse is the work of the commentary stage, when the expansions for a
book are being written and it is known which verses need them. The bases above
are enough to begin.
