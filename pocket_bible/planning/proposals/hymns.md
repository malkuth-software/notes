# Hymns — a short canon of about fifty (U006 A)

CANDIDATE list; see `README.md` in this directory for status and the licence
test. Fifty-six are named so that the owner can strike to about fifty.

How they were chosen: (1) the text is public domain by the test in the README;
(2) the hymn stands on a named passage of scripture, so that it can be linked
from the page; (3) together they cover the day (morning, evening), the
Christian year, the rites of U003 (the Supper, baptism, marriage, burial), and
the Psalms; (4) the two families of U002 are both present — the synagogue's
hymns as well as the church's.

Melodies (U009 B: printed in a simple notation). The tune is named with its
composer and date because the melody has its own term. A tune that fails the
test is marked, with an older tune in its place.

Sources to audit when a row is kept (none fetched): the Open Hymnal Project
(states that its texts and settings are public domain; melodies in ABC, a
plain-text notation — a candidate for the notation of N18); *Hymns Ancient and
Modern* (1861, 1875); *The English Hymnal* (1906) for texts only; *The Hymnal*
of the Episcopal Church (1916); the Scottish Psalter (1650); Singer's *Daily
Prayer Book* (1890) for the Hebrew hymns with English.

**Left out on purpose:** every hymn by Martin Luther, following the owner's
“but not luther!” (O004 note). That note was about philosophical writers; if it
does not reach hymns, say so and the obvious one returns.
