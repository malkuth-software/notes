## Status of everything in this directory

- Every entry is a **CANDIDATE**. Nothing here is admitted.
- **Nothing was fetched.** The handoff says so; these lists exist to be cut.
- Each entry says what it is, why it would earn a place, and its source and
  licence. The licence column is **as understood from general knowledge and
  not verified**; a row the owner keeps goes through the same audit as the
  first wave (statement saved, file pinned) before any text enters.
- No text of any work is quoted. Titles and first lines are used as names.
- Dates of authors and translators are from general reference and are part of
  what the audit re-checks. The working test for a text in English is:
  published before 1931 (public domain in the United States in 2026) **and**
  the author or translator died before 1956 (public domain where the term is
  life plus seventy years). An entry that passes only one of the two says so.

## How to answer

Strike a line, add a line, or write a note beside it — in the file, or by
entry number in the next interview round. A struck entry is kept, marked
struck, with the date; absence is a decision and its record is kept.

## What these lists do to the size of the work

All of this arrives after the first public version (O008 B, U011 B). Counted
roughly, the proposals name about 56 hymns, 40 prayers and creeds, 10 homilies,
9 rules and letters, 28 later writers, and some 30 rabbinic passages. That is a
second book. The charter's risk R7 stands: the review is the owner's time.
