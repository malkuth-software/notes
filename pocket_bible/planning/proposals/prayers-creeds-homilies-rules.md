# Creeds, prayers, homilies, pastoral letters and rules (U007 A–D)

CANDIDATE list; see `README.md` in this directory. Nothing fetched; licences as
understood. “BCP” is the Book of Common Prayer (1549, and the American 1928);
“Singer” is the *Authorised Daily Prayer Book*, 1890, Hebrew and English; “ANF”
and “NPNF” are the Ante-Nicene and Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers series
(1885–1900). All four are audited as sources in `rite-sources.md`.

Blocked or doubtful: the “Peace Prayer” attributed to Francis is anonymous,
French, 1912, and not his; its English forms date from the 1920s and 1930s —
usable if a pre-1931 printing is found, and it must not be credited to Francis.
The Serenity Prayer (Niebuhr, 1930s–40s) is under dispute and left out.
