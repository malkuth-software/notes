  text is a sacred-name Bible the owner will identify (E008).

  a study of metallic rendering.

## 4. Consequences I draw (HYPOTHESIS until tested)

- **C1 Pages can be made at build time.** R020 A fixes page proportions, R018 A
  makes turns instant, K017 wants opening to feel instant. Page breaks then do
  not depend on the window and can be computed per face and size step when the
  container is built: opening a page is a lookup, page numbers are citable, the
  reader's machine never paginates. (J002, J003.)
- **C2 The compositor's scope narrows.** One column per page, no hyphenation,
  no Syriac shaping if Y001 A, at most two texts at once (R012 A), facing-page
  study form rather than verse-aligned columns. Total-fit line breaking is still
  needed (R006 note). The proving slice changes its third passage (Y005, Y006).
- **C3 “Semantic search” is settled in kind.** S006 A, B, C, F: the original
  word behind the English, lexicon domains, cross-references, authored topic
  tags. No vectors unless they win on judged queries (S016 C). No fore-edge
  density strip (S009 A only). The golden workload has one query (V010).
- **C4 Authored text now dominates the product:** expansions, merged lexicon
  entries, key terms, the rendition, translations of Syriac, Aramaic, rabbinic
  Hebrew and Greek deuterocanon, rite explanations. All of it runs under one
  protocol (two models, stored evidence, owner tie-breaks) and all of it is
  source files in the repository. The protocol document is generalized.
- **C5 The gold needs data that does not exist in the open:** a verse-by-verse
  speaker table for the Old Testament. Words of Christ can come from the World
  English Bible's markup (public domain) carried across by verse and word.
- **C6 Controls for the compression study** are raw UTF-8, zstd and xz only
  (K016); PPMd and context-mixing bounds were not chosen.
- **C7 Memory is kept while hidden** (I007 B). Idle is still zero work (I004 A).
